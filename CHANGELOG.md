## 0.2.0

- Excluded projects: keep a project's file and project names off Discord,
  from the status bar menu ("Hide project", or add folders with the folder
  picker and patterns under "Excluded Projects…") or with folder paths,
  globs or project names in the settings. An include list does the
  opposite, and excluded projects can also hide your status completely.
- The status bar shows a Discord icon instead of text, and clicking it
  opens a menu to turn the plugin and privacy mode on or off, hide the
  current project, choose what to show, and reconnect.
- New command: Discord Presence: Show Menu.
- Show Elapsed Time off now keeps the timer at 0:00. Discord always shows
  a timer, and without a start time it counted from the last change.

## 0.1.0

- First release: shows the open file, project, language and elapsed time in
  your Discord status, with settings to hide each, a privacy mode, Running,
  Debugging, Testing and Idle statuses, "Get Lumide" and "Join Lumide
  Discord" buttons (or your own), reconnection when Discord starts or
  restarts, and a status bar item. Works without setup through the
  plugin's own Discord application.
